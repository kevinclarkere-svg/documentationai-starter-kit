## Overview

ParentSearch provides a safe space for adult children to search for time capsules created by their parents. These capsules contain video messages, letters, photos, and keepsakes preserved privately until you're ready. Use the search form on the [Parent Portal](https://parentsearch.org/login) to find potential matches by name or location.

<Callout kind="info">
  Searches are anonymous and private. No personal data is required upfront, and results only reveal capsules when you confirm a match.
</Callout>

## Step-by-Step Search Process

Follow these steps to locate a time capsule.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In or Access Public Search" icon="log-in">
    Visit [parentsearch.org](https://parentsearch.org). No account is needed for initial searches, but create one for secure access to capsules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Search Details" icon="search">
    Use the search bar to input a parent's first name, last name, or partial name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Matches" icon="list">
    Browse anonymized previews like creation date and general location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request Access" icon="key">
    Select a match and submit a verification request. ParentSearch notifies the capsule owner securely.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Narrowing Your Search

Refine results for better accuracy.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="By Name" icon="user">
    Enter a full or partial name. For example, searching `John Smith` shows capsules from parents with matching or similar names.
    
    Partial matches help if you only know a first name.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="By City" icon="map-pin">
    After name search, select a city or state from the filters. This narrows to capsules created from that area.
    
    Combine with name for precise results, like `Mary` in `Seattle`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Advanced Filters" icon="filter">
    Use date ranges or keywords from memories shared with you.
    
    Filters update results in real-time without resubmitting.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Accessing Private Content

Once a parent confirms your identity, gain access to the full time capsule.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Receive Confirmation" icon="mail">
    Get an email notification when the parent approves your request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Capsule" icon="gift">
    Log in to see videos, letters, and photos in a secure viewer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond Safely" icon="message-circle">
    Reply directly through the platform or download content for personal keepsakes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  All communications stay within ParentSearch. You control visibility and can pause access anytime.
</Callout>

## Privacy and Safety Features

ParentSearch prioritizes your security.

<Expandable title="How We Protect Your Data" default-open="true">
  Searches don't log IP addresses or require personal info. Capsules remain encrypted until mutual verification. You can report suspicious activity anonymously.
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What If No Matches?">
  No results? Try broader terms or contact support. Many capsules use nicknames or locations from the time of separation.
</Expandable>

<Callout kind="alert">
  Never share verification details outside the platform. Report any pressure to do so.
</Callout>
