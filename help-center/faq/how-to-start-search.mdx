## Overview

To begin searching for biological parents, sign up and create your profile.

## Steps

1. Go to `https://parentsearch.com/signup`
2. Fill in your personal details and family info
3. Submit any known information about your birth or adoption
4. Click "Start Search"

ParentSearch will begin scanning databases for possible matches. Results may appear in minutes.
