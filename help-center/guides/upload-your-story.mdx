## Why upload your story?

Stories help connect with relatives and improve match accuracy.

## How to Share

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your profile">
    Go to `https://parentsearch.com` and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to 'My Story'">
    Click 'Profile', then select 'My Story'.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and upload files">
    Add your background, any memories, and upload supporting documents or pictures. Click 'Save'.
  </Step>
</Steps>
