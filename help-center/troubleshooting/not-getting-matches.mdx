---
title: Not Getting Matches
description: What to do if no parent matches appear.
---

## Symptoms

- Your search returns no results
- No matches found notification

## Possible Reasons

- Limited info in your profile
- Database updates in progress

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update your profile">
    Add more details: birth location, date, known relatives, or photos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try again later">
    Matches improve as the database updates. Check back after 24 hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>
