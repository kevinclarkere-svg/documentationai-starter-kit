## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:

<Callout kind="info">
- A valid email address for account verification
- Access to a modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- A stable internet connection
</Callout>

<Callout kind="tip">
No software installation required. Everything runs in your browser.
</Callout>

## Create Your Account

Sign up quickly to access the parent portal or start searching.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Signup Page" icon="external-link">
    Go to the [join page](https://parentsearch.org/join).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Details">
    Provide your first name, last name, email, and create a secure password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Email">
    Check your inbox for a verification link and click it to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Profile">
    Add optional details like city or birthdate to help with searches.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Log In to the Portal

Access your dashboard securely.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Login" icon="login">
    Head to the [parent portal login](https://parentsearch.org/login).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Credentials">
    Input your email and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Two-Factor Authentication" icon="shield">
    If enabled, enter the code sent to your email or authenticator app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your First Actions

Choose your path based on your role.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Parents: Build Time Capsule" icon="heart">

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Start New Capsule" icon="plus">
        Click "Create Time Capsule" on your dashboard.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Upload Content">
        Add videos, letters, photos, and notes. Keep messages heartfelt and positive.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set Privacy">
        Review settings to ensure content stays private until searched.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save and Preview">
        Save your capsule and preview how it will appear to your child.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Children: Perform Search" icon="search">

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Enter Name" icon="user">
        On the homepage, type your parent's first name or part of their last name.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Narrow by City">
        Optionally select a city on the next screen for better results.
      </Step>
      <Step title="View Matches">
        Browse potential matches and click to view capsule previews if available.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Contact Safely">
        Use the secure messaging to connect when ready.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

  </Tab>
</Tabs>
